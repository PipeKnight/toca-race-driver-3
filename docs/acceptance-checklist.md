# TOCA Race Driver 3 Recompilation Acceptance Checklist

The first release target is the North American PS2 disc `SLUS-21182` v1.02. Players supply their own disc image or extracted disc files. Close the Windows 11 milestone when sections 1 and 2 pass on a release candidate. Advertise each additional platform or disc revision after its own verification in section 3.
